Daily Quran Articles

Short reminders and helpful articles about Quran learning, Tajweed and Islamic teachings.

ARTICLE 01

The Importance of Learning the Quran

Learning the Quran is a journey of faith, knowledge and daily practice. Begin with small, consistent steps and seek guidance from a qualified teacher.

ARTICLE 02

Why Is Tajweed Important?

Tajweed helps us recite the Quran correctly by giving each letter its proper sound and articulation. Regular practice can gradually improve recitation.

ARTICLE 03

Make Quran Learning a Daily Habit

Choose a manageable time each day, revise what you have learned, and keep learning with patience and consistency.
