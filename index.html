TAJWEED & QURAN

Why Is Tajweed Important?

Tajweed helps us recite the Holy Quran with correct pronunciation and care. It teaches us to give each Arabic letter its proper sound and articulation point. Learning Tajweed helps avoid pronunciation mistakes and improves the clarity of our recitation.

LEARNING TAJWEED

Why Should We Learn Tajweed?

Learning Tajweed gives us the knowledge and practice needed to recite the Quran correctly. It helps learners recognize Arabic letters, understand pronunciation rules and improve through regular practice with a qualified teacher.

QURAN RECITATION

Is It Permissible to Recite the Quran Without Tajweed?

Muslims should strive to recite the Quran correctly. Scholars distinguish between essential pronunciation mistakes that may change a letter or meaning and finer rules that improve recitation. Learn and correct mistakes as much as possible, and ask a qualified Quran teacher or scholar about specific cases.

DAILY PRACTICE

How Can We Improve Our Tajweed?

Set aside a little time each day to practice Quran recitation. Listen carefully to a qualified reciter, repeat the verses and ask a teacher to correct your pronunciation. Consistent practice and revision can help you improve step by step.
